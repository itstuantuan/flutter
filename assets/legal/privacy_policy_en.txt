Pals Privacy Policy
Pals is operated by Beijing Yunshang Qulv Technology Co., Ltd. (“we”). This Policy explains how we collect, use, store, protect, and provide your personal information to others when you use Pals, and how you may exercise your personal information rights. Our personal information protection contact is contact@viitor.top.
Pals offers text and voice conversations with the AI friend Tony, interest selection and content exploration, viewing discovery details and sources, observing AI interactions, viewing AI friend profiles, and continuing conversations when you return. This Policy applies to these services in the Pals application. Third-party websites you choose to open are subject to their own privacy rules.
Please pay particular attention to voice information, conversation context and inferred interests, third-party AI services, and data storage and deletion. We process personal information on lawful grounds under applicable law, such as consent, necessity to perform a contract to which you are a party, or fulfillment of legal obligations. Where consent or separate consent is required, we will obtain it as required by law. Reading this Policy or continuing to use the application does not replace legally required authorization.
I Personal Information We Collect and Use
1 Account Sign-in and Profile Display
When you sign in using a phone verification code, we process the country or region code, phone number, verification code and verification result, user identifier, and necessary sign-in records you provide. These are used to send codes, verify account ownership, maintain your session, and protect account security. Without a phone number or completed verification, you cannot complete the current phone sign-in process. We will not ask you to provide verification codes to customer support or Tony.
The name or nickname you enter lets Tony address you and allows us to display your account profile in the application. Avoid using unrelated information, such as an identity number or home address, as your nickname. Displaying your profile in the application does not mean it is made public to other users. Current AI friend and interaction entries do not authorize disclosure of your personal profile.
2 Text Conversations and Ongoing Communication with Tony
When you send text, we process your message, its sending time, Tony's reply, and related conversation identifiers to generate replies, save conversations, and continue communication. To resume an earlier topic, we use historical messages and topic summaries relevant to the current conversation, together with interests and preferences you have expressed. This information may also be used for return greetings and “continue the conversation” cards.
Opening chat or expanding the input box does not automatically send a message. Unsent text may be temporarily saved when you leave a page so you can resume typing. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of conversations and related preference information through the channels in Section IX.
3 Voice Input and Voice Replies
After you actively tap the microphone, grant permission, and begin voice input, we process the audio for that input and the recognized text to convert your speech into text and generate a reply. Section IV describes whether recognition occurs locally or in the cloud, the actual recipients, and processing regions. Denying microphone permission prevents voice input but does not prevent text chat.
Voice replies use the reply text that Tony will read aloud for speech synthesis and playback. Playing sound on your device does not itself require recording your voice. Microphone permission is not treated as authorization for continuous listening or background recording. Permission for ordinary voice input is not extended to voiceprint identification or voice cloning.
4 Interest Selection and Content Exploration
You may select multiple interests from music, games, film and television, anime, food, travel, reading, trending topics, and pets. We process your confirmed selections, changes, and exploration requests to match discovery content and reuse your choices in later explorations. Interests you explicitly express in chat may also be extracted as default selections on the interest page. You may remove or adjust them before confirming.
We use selected interests, conversation context relevant to the current recommendation, and exploration results to generate discoveries, recommendation explanations, and home cards. Inferred interests may be inaccurate and do not constitute judgments about your real identity, health, or other sensitive characteristics. You can change selections through “Adjust interests.” To correct inferences, delete preference information, or decline recommendations based on personal characteristics, contact contact@viitor.top. We will handle the request under law and explain any effect on exploration. Adjusting interests does not delete all chat history.
5 Discovery Details and AI Interactions
When you open discoveries, observe AI discussions, or view AI friend profiles, we process the corresponding content, topic or AI identifier, and records necessary to maintain browsing state. This lets us display details, retain your page position, and continue conversations. After you select “Chat with Tony,” content related to the discovery, discussion, or friend is included as conversation context. Tony replies after you confirm sending.
These entries open your private chat with Tony. They do not automatically publish your views to the original AI discussion or send a direct message to that AI friend. AI friend lists, profiles, and interaction records are AI interaction content in the product and do not mean that we have read your phone contacts. Merely observing or continuing a conversation does not cause us to show your private chat records to other users.
II Operational Information, Customer Support, and Sensitive Personal Information
1 Operational and Security Information
To maintain sign-in, protect service security, and troubleshoot problems, we process device model, operating system and version, application version, IP address, network status, request times, sign-in status, errors or crashes, and feature-operation records related to a fault, within the necessary scope.
If device storage or similar technology saves your session, preferences, unsent text, or page position, it is used only for the corresponding function. If technology for analytics, cross-application tracking, or advertising is introduced separately, we will explain the actual information types, recipients, and choices before enabling it and obtain required authorization. This Policy does not pre-authorize that processing.
2 Customer Support and Rights Requests
When you contact us by email, we process your contact email, information necessary to locate your account, your description of the problem, and attachments you voluntarily provide. These are used to respond, investigate problems, and handle personal information requests. Provide only material relevant to your request. Do not send passwords, verification codes, or unrelated complete chat records.
3 Sensitive Information That Conversations May Contain
Chats and voice input may contain health information, financial account details, specific identity information, precise location, or other sensitive personal information you voluntarily provide. Disclosure or misuse of such information may affect your dignity, personal safety, or property security. Ordinary interest exploration and chat do not require this information. Avoid entering it, and do not submit others' personal information without lawful authorization.
Where processing sensitive personal information is genuinely necessary, we will explain the specific purpose, necessity, and effects on your rights and obtain required authorization, including separate consent where applicable. Sending a chat does not constitute consent to use its sensitive information for unrelated recommendations, public display, or model training. Tony's replies are not professional diagnoses or authoritative judgments about your actual circumstances.
III Device Permissions and Your Choices
1 Microphone
When you actively use voice input and system authorization is needed, we request microphone permission to capture that input. You may deny or disable permission and continue using text. Merely browsing discoveries, observing AI interactions, or playing a welcome narration will not be used as a reason to record your voice.
2 Notifications
During exploration, you may choose whether to receive alerts about new discoveries. After you actively choose to enable them, we request system notification permission and process the device push identifier, authorization status, and necessary delivery records. Choosing “Not now” or denying notifications does not affect exploration or viewing discoveries. The system may show notification previews on the lock screen; you can adjust their visibility in system settings. You can manage Pals microphone and notification permissions in your device settings; menus depend on your device. System permission and consent to process personal information are independent. Revoking a permission does not automatically withdraw other consent or delete earlier data. This Policy does not grant current features access to contacts, precise location, photos, or the camera.
IV AI Processing and Third-party Services
1 Information Included in AI Requests
To generate Tony's replies, we process the text or recognized speech you currently send and relevant context necessary for the conversation. This may include your preferred name, related historical messages or summaries, interests and preferences, the current discovery, discussion topic, or AI friend profile. We limit the information sent according to the request rather than indiscriminately providing every account's data to all providers merely because AI is integrated.
The currently integrated model is named GPT-luna. This name does not itself identify the provider's legal entity or data-processing region. The specific supplier, received content, and processing rules must be disclosed in this section's list. Before providing personal information to a third-party AI service, we will explain the recipient, information types, purpose, and method and obtain legally required permission, including separate consent where required. Refusing this processing may make the corresponding AI feature unavailable but does not affect features that do not depend on it.
2 Whether Conversations and Voice Are Used for Training
We do not use your private conversations, audio, or recognized speech to train or fine-tune general-purpose AI models. This content is used to provide replies, maintain conversation context, and deliver the interest-exploration services you choose.
3 Third-party Service Disclosures
We call necessary APIs or SDKs according to the feature. Entrusted processors should process only necessary information as agreed. If a third party independently determines processing purposes and methods, we fulfill legally required notice and separate-consent procedures for providing personal information to it. AI model service: GPT-luna. Its purposes are conversation replies, understanding relevant context, and explaining content recommendations. Its information scope is the necessary content described in item 1 of this section.
Voice services provide speech recognition and reply synthesis. Their information scope is, respectively, the current audio and information needed for recognition, and the reply text to be read aloud. SMS and account authentication services send verification codes and verify sign-in, using the dialing code, phone number, and necessary sending and verification records. Cloud hosting and data storage services operate the application backend and store accounts, conversations, and necessary exploration records.
V Provision to Others, Transfers, and Public Disclosure
We do not sell your personal information. Provision to others is limited to the purposes and necessary scope stated in this Policy and requires a lawful basis. Current private chat does not become a public community-posting feature because of AI interaction scenes.
Entrusted processing: Providers of model inference, voice, verification codes, hosting, storage, push notifications, and similar services must process information as agreed. We specify processing purposes, duration, methods, information types, security measures, and each party's responsibilities and supervise as required by law. When the entrusted relationship ends, information is returned or deleted under law and the agreement.
Independent third parties: Where personal information must be provided to a recipient that independently determines purposes and methods, we disclose its name, contact details, purposes, methods, and information types and obtain separate consent as required by law, unless otherwise provided by law.
Corporate changes: If personal information must be transferred due to a merger, division, dissolution, bankruptcy, or similar event, we disclose the recipient's name and contact details as required by law and require continued protection obligations. If processing purposes or methods change, corresponding consent is obtained again as required by law.
Legal circumstances: When fulfilling legal obligations or responding to lawful requests from competent authorities, we verify the basis and limit the scope of disclosure. Public disclosure requires separate consent or another statutory basis. Your private chat with Tony cannot automatically be treated as information you have made public.
VI Third-party Content and External Links
Selecting entries such as “View source” may take you to websites or applications we do not operate. Third parties may process your IP address, browsing records, and information you voluntarily provide on their pages under their own rules. Their sign-in, playback, subscription, or other requirements are subject to their notices. Read their privacy policies before providing information.
Opening a source link does not authorize us to give that website your phone number or all conversations with Tony. If Pals actively sends additional personal information through embedded pages or technical components, we must still explain the specific recipient and scope and fulfill the corresponding legal obligations. An external link does not exempt us from those obligations.
VII Storage Locations, Retention, and Cross-border Processing
1 Storage Locations and Cross-border Arrangements
The primary storage and backup regions for accounts, conversations, audio, exploration records, and logs, and the countries or regions where AI and voice processing occur, are within China.
If personal information is provided outside China, including through remote access by overseas recipients, we will first disclose the recipient's name and contact details, purposes, methods, information types, retention rules, and ways to exercise rights, obtain required separate consent, and complete applicable cross-border personal information procedures. Accepting this Policy is not blanket authorization for all cross-border processing.
2 Retention and Deletion
We retain information only for the shortest period necessary to achieve the processing purpose or the period required by law. Account information maintains the account; conversations, related summaries, and preferences support ongoing communication; exploration and browsing state display results and restore pages; verification codes, operational logs, and push records support verification, security, or delivery.
When a purpose is fulfilled, a retention period expires, or statutory deletion conditions apply, we delete or anonymize the information. Where retention is legally required or deletion is temporarily technically difficult, we stop processing except for storage and necessary security protection. Backups are cleared on applicable cycles, and confirmed deletion requirements remain in effect when a backup is restored. Copies held by entrusted processors are coordinated for handling under law. Uninstalling the application does not itself delete an account or server data.
VIII Personal Information Security
We adopt reasonable technical and administrative measures according to data types and risks, including access controls, authentication, protection during transmission and storage, backups, and necessary log audits. Personnel and providers may access personal information only within the scope needed for their duties. “Private chat” is not described as a guarantee of end-to-end encryption or that no one can access it. Actual processing remains subject to this Policy and applicable law.
If a leak, alteration, loss, or similar incident occurs or may occur, we take remedial measures, report to authorities as legally required, and notify affected users as required by law. Keep your device and sign-in credentials secure. When contacting support, obscure unrelated personal information where possible.
IX Managing Your Personal Information
1 Access, Correction, Copies, and Deletion
You may request access to, copies of, correction or supplementation of, or deletion of your personal information, withdraw consent, restrict or refuse relevant processing, and request explanations of processing rules as provided by law. Where statutory conditions are met, you may request information transfer. You may also exercise legally available rights to explanations of or refusal of automated decisions through the email below.
You can change selected interests in “Adjust interests” and manage permissions in system settings. If a self-service entry is unavailable, or you need to delete chats, summaries, inferred preferences, audio, or other records, email contact@viitor.top with “Pals Personal Information Request,” specifying the type and scope of your request. We will not require you to first use a feature that the application does not yet provide.
2 Account Deletion and Data Deletion Requests
An in-app self-service deletion entry is not currently a prerequisite for making a request. You may email contact@viitor.top with the subject “Pals Account Deletion and Data Deletion Request,” provide a user identifier or registered phone number sufficient to locate your account, and explain your request. If you only want some information deleted, specify its scope. Deleting a single item does not require deletion of your entire account.
We verify account ownership through necessary and reasonable means and do not request passwords or ask you to send verification codes to customer support. After verification, we will act or provide an explanation within 15 working days, or within a shorter period required by applicable law. If further verification or supplier coordination is needed, we explain the reason and expected completion time rather than delaying indefinitely.
After account deletion, you can no longer access its content through that account. We delete or anonymize associated information under law, including profiles, conversations, related summaries and preferences, audio requiring deletion, and exploration records. Information that must legally be retained or remains in backups is handled under Section VII, with corresponding limitations explained. Information processed by entrusted providers is coordinated for handling as well. Before deleting your account, consider whether you need a copy of your personal information under law.
3 Withdrawal of Consent and Feedback
You may revoke permissions through system settings and withdraw consent for relevant personal information processing through the email above. Withdrawal does not affect the validity of earlier processing based on consent, but related features may no longer function. Disabling permissions, deleting conversations, deleting preferences, and deleting an account are distinct actions. We handle your actual request and do not substitute one action for all requests.
If we cannot fulfill a request, we explain the reasons and available remedies. You may also complain to the competent regulator or protect your rights through other lawful channels.
